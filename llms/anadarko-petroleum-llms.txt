# Anadarko Petroleum

> Anadarko Petroleum Corporation was one of the world's largest independent oil and natural gas
> exploration and production companies, operating across the United States onshore (Wattenberg Field
> in Colorado, the Delaware Basin in Texas), the deepwater Gulf of Mexico, and international assets
> in Mozambique and Ghana. Occidental Petroleum acquired Anadarko in August 2019 in a transaction
> valued at roughly $55 billion. Anadarko no longer exists as an operating company.

## Status: defunct / absorbed

Anadarko published no public developer API, no developer portal, no SDK and no machine-readable
API contract while it operated, and it publishes none now. Its technology integrations were
enterprise systems shared with partners and joint-venture operators, not a public API program.

- The legacy corporate domain `anadarko.com` still holds a DNS A record (a CSC brand-protection
  holding address) but serves nothing: both port 80 and port 443 refuse connections, and
  `www.anadarko.com` does not resolve at all.
- The successor site is Occidental Petroleum at https://www.oxy.com. Occidental is a separate
  company with its own profile; nothing served from `oxy.com` is attributed to Anadarko here.
- No `/.well-known/` document, no `llms.txt`, no OpenAPI, no GraphQL, no MCP server and no A2A
  agent card was found on either host. See `well-known/anadarko-petroleum-well-known.yml` for the
  full probe record with HTTP statuses.
- No first-party client library exists on npm, PyPI, Maven Central, NuGet, RubyGems, crates.io,
  Packagist or pkg.go.dev.
- No GitHub organization exists under `anadarko`, `anadarko-petroleum` or `oxy`.
